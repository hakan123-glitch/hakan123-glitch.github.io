Privacy Policy – Babamın Defteri Uploader

This application is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Babamın Defteri" to upload and schedule videos on that one channel.

  • It accesses only the owner's own YouTube account, through Google's official YouTube Data API, with the owner's permission.

  • The login token is stored only on the owner's own computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • No data is transferred to third parties.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
